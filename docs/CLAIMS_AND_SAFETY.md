# Claims and Safety

## Claims

Mission is not efficacy evidence.

Acceptable before field evidence:

> Designed to help people clarify meaningful goals, take deliberate action, record outcomes, and learn from what happens.

Do not promise achievement, wealth, success, improved health, or other outcomes without appropriate evidence.

Patterns derived from recorded history must be phrased as observations unless causality is actually established. Recommendations must be able to express uncertainty and explain why they appeared.

## Safety boundaries

Daily Aim is a general-purpose reflection and action tool. It may help a person clarify, organize, reflect, prepare questions, and track actions.

It is not an emergency service and must not present itself as a medical, mental-health, legal, financial, or physical-safety authority. High-stakes contexts require conservative language and appropriate qualified human help where needed.

Persistence must never be framed as a command to continue harmful, unsafe, illegal, or evidence-invalidated behavior.
